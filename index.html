Rules of the ring

  • High moves whiff against a crouch.
  • Mid moves beat a crouching guard; block them standing.
  • Low moves must be blocked crouching (down + back).
  • Hold back to block. Tap forward twice to dash, back twice to backstep.
  • Throws (1+3 or 2+4) beat blocks. Press the matching 1 or 2 fast to break.
  • A hit while your opponent is mid-attack is a counter hit: more damage, more stun.
  • Below 25% health you enter Rage and hit harder.
